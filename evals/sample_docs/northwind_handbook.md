# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

This handbook describes how we work at Northwind Labs, a fictional software company used as sample data for DocPilot. It covers working hours, leave, benefits, engineering practices and security.

## 1. Working model

Northwind Labs follows a hybrid working model. Employees work from the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and may work remotely on Monday and Friday. Teams can agree on additional office days for planning weeks or launches.

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours whenever possible. Outside core hours, employees choose their own schedule as long as their work and commitments are met.

Employees may work remotely from outside India for up to 30 days per calendar year, subject to written approval from their manager and the People team.

## 2. Leave policy

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid leave per calendar year. Paid leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service. Unused paid leave can be carried forward, up to a maximum of 10 days, into the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

Employees also receive 12 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Parental leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks of fully paid leave for the secondary caregiver. Parental leave can be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption of a child.

Leave requests should be submitted in the PeopleDesk portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave of 5 days or more.

## 3. Learning and development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of ₹60,000 per year. The budget covers courses, certifications, conference tickets and books. Unused learning budget does not roll over. Requests are submitted through the LearnHub form and approved by the employee's manager.

Engineers are also encouraged to spend up to 4 hours every two weeks on learning during working time.

## 4. Equipment and home office

New employees choose either a MacBook Pro 14 or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon as their work laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier if the device fails.

Every employee receives a one-time home office stipend of ₹25,000 for a chair, desk, monitor or other setup items. The stipend must be claimed within the first 6 months of joining.

## 5. Expenses and travel

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Receipts are mandatory for every expense above ₹500. Expenses are approved by the employee's manager and reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

For business travel, employees book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class may be approved for flights of 6 hours or longer. The hotel limit is ₹7,500 per night in metro cities and ₹5,000 per night elsewhere.

## 6. Engineering practices

Every pull request needs at least one approval before merging. Changes to the payments service need two approvals, one of which must come from a member of the payments team. Pull requests should ideally stay under 400 changed lines so they can be reviewed carefully.

All services must have automated tests in CI, and the main branch must always be deployable. Production deployments happen through the release pipeline only; manual changes to production are not allowed.

## 7. On-call

Engineering teams run a weekly on-call rotation that starts on Monday at 10:00 and ends the following Monday at 10:00. Engineers on call receive an on-call allowance of ₹4,000 per week.

A P1 incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and must have a published status update within 30 minutes. Every P1 incident requires a blameless postmortem within 5 working days.

## 8. Performance and growth

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback and a manager assessment, followed by calibration across teams. Promotions are decided once a year, during the April cycle.

## 9. Probation and notice period

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation, the notice period is 30 days. After probation is confirmed, the notice period is 60 days for both the employee and the company.

## 10. Referrals

Employees who refer a successful candidate receive a referral bonus. The bonus is ₹75,000 for engineering hires and ₹40,000 for all other roles. The bonus is paid after the new hire completes 90 days at Northwind Labs.

## 11. Security

Two-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts. Passwords must be stored in the company password manager. Customer data must never be stored on personal devices.

Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery, even if the employee is unsure whether it is a real incident.
